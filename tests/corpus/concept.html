A <tokenizer> for AGNOS

vyakarana takes source bytes and yields (kind, start, len) spans.

What it does

Reads bytes, classifies them, emits tokens. That's the entire job — no rendering, no parsing past the lexical layer.

  • 29 bundled grammars at v1.6.0.
  • Zero-copy tokens: (kind, start, len).
  • Coverage invariant: every byte accounted for.
